CONCEPT — WORK IN PROGRESS

MOODYPLAYER

A concept for a mood-aware listening experience: a scroll-driven opening that leads into an optional camera step, where a model running locally in the browser reads an expression — the idea being to answer it with an ambient soundscape synthesised live. An exploration of Next.js, on-device inference and the Web Audio API.

01 NEXT.JS · GSAP · FACE-API · WEB AUDIO API

UNIVERSITY TEAM PROJECT — EARLY MVP

SKILLGAPAI

An exploration of AI-assisted job preparation, designed around a simple flow: upload a resume, paste a job description, and have a model compare the two to surface skill gaps and suggest ATS-friendly improvements. Prototyped as a university team project on a MERN stack with LLMs and PDF parsing.

02 MERN STACK · LLMs · PDF PARSING

EXPLORATION — WORK IN PROGRESS

TALKING RABBITT

A conversational approach to business intelligence. Upload a CSV, ask questions in plain English, and explore your data through structured analysis and visual insights. The project explores how generative AI can make data analysis more accessible without requiring users to write queries manually.
